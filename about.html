About Plinth Renovations and Construction

Goodwood-based renovators serving Greater Cape Town with honest scopes, fixed prices and tidy sites.

Built on vision, crafted with precision.

Plinth started with a simple frustration: vague quotes, moving prices and messy sites. We do the opposite, written scope, fixed price, daily tidy, photo updates.

Based at 64 Oxford Street, Richmond Estate, Goodwood, we specialise in the homes that make up most of Cape Town: facebrick and plaster 70s–90s houses, townhouses, flats and small commercial lets. We handle small handyman lists through to full kitchen, bathroom and exterior renovations, with registered electricians / plumbers where CoCs are needed.

  • Owner-supervised crews
  • Coastal-correct materials (stainless, treated timber, flexible waterproofing)
  • 12-month workmanship warranty on renovations
