02Why Plinth

Built for Cape Town. Not a Generic Contractor.

We are Plinth Renovations and Construction, based at 64 Oxford Street, Richmond Estate, Goodwood. After years working on homes across the Northern Suburbs and the greater city, we build for what most contractors underestimate: this climate.

At the foot of Table Mountain your home takes a unique beating. Atlantic salt air corrodes fixtures and fasteners. The summer Cape Doctor drives dust and UV into paint and timber. Winter brings sideways rain that finds every failed seal, flat roof and cracked parapet. Hard municipal water scales geysers and taps, and older Goodwood, Parow and Milnerton stock often hides damp, cracked plaster and tired electrics.

Our playbook is written for that: stainless or treated fixings near the coast, flexible waterproofing for parapets and flat roofs, mould-resistant prep, and honest advice on where to spend and where to save.

  • Free written, flat-rate estimates, no hourly surprises
  • Respect for occupied homes: dust control, daily tidy, clear timelines
  • Supplier-grade materials suited to coastal Western Cape

Do you offer handyman services in Goodwood and surrounds? +

Yes. We're based in Richmond Estate, Goodwood and serve Greater Cape Town, Bellville, Parow, Century City, Milnerton, Table View, Claremont, Sea Point and more. See service areas.

How much does a handyman cost in Cape Town? +

Small repairs are typically quoted flat-rate after photos or a walkthrough. Every job gets a free written estimate, the number on the estimate is the number you pay.

What areas do you serve? +

Greater Cape Town within ~25km of Goodwood, plus Stellenbosch / Somerset West by arrangement for larger renovations.

Are you insured? +

Yes, we work insured, provide written scopes, and offer a 12-month workmanship warranty on renovations. Certified electrical / plumbing via registered subcontractors with CoCs where required.
