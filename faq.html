FAQ

Straight answers on price, timelines and guarantees.

How much does a handyman cost in Cape Town?

We price flat-rate, not hourly. Send photos via the estimate form for a fixed written price. Small fixes start from a few hundred rand; room renovations are scoped on site.

Do you charge for estimates?

No, written estimates are free in our core service area.

How fast can you start?

Punch lists: 3 to 7 business days. Renovations: 1 to 3 weeks depending on materials. Winter waterproofing books out Feb–May, book early.

Do you guarantee work?

Yes, 12-month workmanship warranty on renovations. Manufacturer warranties apply to geysers, waterproofing systems, etc.

Do you handle electrical / plumbing CoCs?

For work requiring certification we use registered subcontractors and arrange CoCs at cost.

Which areas do you serve?

Greater Cape Town from our Goodwood base, see areas.
